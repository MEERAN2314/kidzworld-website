Our Comprehensive Programs

At International Kidz World Montessori Play School, we offer a range of programs designed to cater to the unique developmental needs of children at different age groups.

Play Group (1.5 - 2.5 years)

A gentle introduction to school life, focusing on sensory exploration, motor skill development, and social interaction. Our play group environment is carefully prepared to encourage independence and a sense of wonder, allowing our youngest learners to explore safely and confidently.

Pre.KG (2.5 - 3.5 years)

Engaging activities designed to build foundational skills in language, math, practical life, and cultural studies. Children in this program develop concentration, coordination, independence, and a love for learning through hands-on materials and guided discovery.

Enroll Now

L.K.G (3.5 - 4.5 years)

Advanced Montessori curriculum preparing children for elementary school with a strong academic and social foundation. This program fosters critical thinking, problem-solving, and leadership skills, ensuring a smooth transition to higher education.

Enroll Now

U.K.G (4.5 - 5.5 years)

Enrichment activities, homework support, and creative play for older children in a safe and engaging environment. Our U.K.G program provides a balanced blend of academic assistance and recreational activities, promoting continued growth and development.
